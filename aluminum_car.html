Aluminum Air Powered Car

This report presents the design, manufacturing, and testing of an air-powered vehicle driven solely by compressed air energy. The vehicle utilizes a nozzle-based propulsion system to convert the stored pressure energy in a 3-liter, 10-bar air tank into kinetic energy that generates thrust. The design process includes fluid, mechanical, and electrical subsystems integration, with a focus on maximizing performance, minimizing weight, and ensuring reliable control and braking. Analytical calculations, CAD modeling, manufacturing procedures, and experimental testing were conducted to validate the design. The final prototype successfully completed the required 10-meter track and applied effective braking, demonstrating the feasibility and efficiency of the proposed design.
