Delta Robot

Designed, manufactured and controlled a 3-DOF Delta Robot, for the first time building a robot with that level of complexity. It was not only about how to control its 3 connected legs, the real challenge was to build it with 2 stepper motors and 1 dc motor for the last leg. Not to mention, that we had to design and implement a soft-gripper; allowing our robot to hold different objects by encapsulating them. It is a team of 5 and I took the challenge to design the whole model, along with designing and building the soft-gripper myself. The mission was to move the robot in a line in its 3D workspace and go to certain coordinates using inverse kinematics
